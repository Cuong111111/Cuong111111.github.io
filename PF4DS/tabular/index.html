03 / FEATURE AUDIT & DATA PROCESSING

Only use what is known at booking time.

All 32 source columns are audited before building the 24-feature matrix.

01

Keep booking-time inputs

Hotel, lead time, requested arrival date, stay length, guest counts, meal, country, market and distribution channels, guest history, reserved room type, deposit type, customer type, and conditionally ADR.

02

Transform identifiers

Convert sparse company to has_company and remove the raw ID. Treat agent as categorical, not continuous. The matrix has 14 numeric and 10 categorical inputs.

03

Exclude future information

Exclude assigned room type, booking changes, waiting-list days, parking spaces, special requests, reservation status, and status date. is_canceled is target-only; status fields reveal the outcome.

04

Retain rows; validate values

All 119,390 rows and valid binary target labels are retained. Convert one negative ADR to missing. Before imputation, missing values remain in agent (16,340), country (488), children (4), and ADR (1). There are 35,435 repeated predictor signatures; matching signatures are grouped into one partition.

ADR timing assumption

ADR is included only if it is the rate known or quoted at booking creation. If it is finalized after the stay, remove it and rerun the workflow.

Preprocessing is learned from training data

Numeric inputs use median imputation and standard scaling. Categorical inputs receive a “Missing” category and one-hot encoding with unknown categories ignored. Every transformation is fitted inside the pipeline on train only.

Training-only scaling diagnostic

Scaling supports Logistic Regression; Random Forest is generally insensitive to monotonic scaling.

07 / INTERPRETATION & LIMITATIONS

Read the result with care.

The revised workflow improves temporal evaluation and leakage control, but does not guarantee production performance.

01

Temporal mix changes

Cancellation rates are 37.98% in train, 40.04% in validation, and 29.59% in test. The later-period shift helps explain why test scores are lower than validation scores.

02

Verify feature availability

The audit assumes prediction at booking creation. ADR is included only if the quoted rate is known then; otherwise remove it and retrain.

03

Duplicates need provenance

Exact duplicate rows remain because the data has no unique booking ID. Grouping signatures across splits prevents a known form of leakage, but does not prove duplicates are valid distinct bookings.

04

Choose thresholds for operations

The 0.50 threshold maximizes validation F1 in this saved run. A hotel should weigh missed cancellations against unnecessary alerts and validate the chosen threshold on future data.

The reported metrics describe one chronological holdout and one model run. They are not causal effects or a production guarantee.
